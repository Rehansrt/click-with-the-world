Fair Play

Each click adds exactly one. Every click is verified by Firebase App Check (using Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise) to block bots and automated clicking, and a short per-browser rate limit stops rapid repeat clicks. Clicks that fail verification are rejected. No system is perfect, but these checks keep the count honest.

See the FAQ or the Privacy Policy for more.

Questions?

Quick answers to common questions.

What is Click With The World?

A single live counter shared by everyone on the internet. Click the button, and the global number goes up by one, for everyone, in real time.

Is it free?

Yes. Clicking is free and needs no sign-up. The site may show ads and accepts optional tips.

How is my country detected?

Your approximate country comes from our hosting provider, Vercel, based on your connection. If that isn't available, your browser asks a third-party service, ipwho.is, for a country code. We don't store your IP address.

Is my data safe?

There are no accounts, and we don't store your IP address, name or email with clicks. A random ID in your browser is used only for rate-limiting. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
