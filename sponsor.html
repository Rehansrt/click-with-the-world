Sponsor FAQ

Common questions about sponsoring.

How does sponsorship work?

Email us or use the inquiry form with the placement and milestone you're interested in. We'll confirm availability and pricing. Sponsors are reviewed before going live.

Where will my logo appear?

On the Home page and the Leaderboard page: in the Top Countries card now, and on the Home page in the Next Milestone card while your milestone is active. Other pages will be added here when they launch.

Can I sponsor a specific milestone?

Yes — see the Milestone Inventory above. Pick an available milestone and use the inquiry form or email us.

How long does a sponsorship last?

You're listed on this page as a partner from the day you join. Your logo appears on the Home page's Next Milestone card from the moment your milestone becomes the next one until it is reached. Top Countries placements run for an agreed period.
