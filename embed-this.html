How it works
What the widget does, and what it uses.

    

    
    
      
  1. The embed is a compact page (just the button and the live count) served from this site inside your iframe, on a compact dark pill, so it stays readable on light or dark pages.
    2. 
      
  2. It reads and writes the same Firebase Realtime Database as the main site, so a click on your embedded widget adds to the same global total and country leaderboard.
    3. 
      
  3. There is a short pause between clicks, to help stop accidental rapid-fire clicking from a single embed.
    4. 
      
  4. It uses Firebase for the live count and Firebase App Check (using Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise) to verify clicks. It finds the visitor's country from our host, Vercel; if that isn't available it asks ipwho.is for a country code. It keeps a random rate-limiting ID in the browser's storage and sets no cookies of its own. It shows no ads. See the Privacy Policy.
    5. 
      
  5. It runs in its own iframe, so it can't affect your page's styles or scripts, and your page can't affect it.
