General

Common questions about Click With The World.

What is Click With The World?

A single live counter shared by everyone on the internet. Click the button, and the global number goes up by one, for everyone, in real time.

Is it free?

Yes. Clicking is free and needs no sign-up. The site may show ads and accepts optional tips.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or sign-ins, and you never need to give your name or email to click.

How is my country detected?

Your approximate country comes from our hosting provider, Vercel, based on your connection. If that isn't available, your browser asks a third-party service, ipwho.is, for a country code. We don't store your IP address. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I click as many times as I want?

There's no overall limit, but there's a short pause of under a second between clicks from the same browser. It's part of the rate-limiting we use to help keep the count fair.

Why did my click not count?

Usually one of these: you clicked again during the short pause between clicks, you were briefly offline, or a browser extension or privacy tool blocked the connection to the live counter. Refresh the page and try again. If it keeps happening, contact us.

Can I put the counter on my website?

Yes, it's free. Choose a size and copy the snippet on the Embed page.

How can I support the project?

You can leave an optional tip on Ko-fi. Payments are handled entirely by Ko-fi, and we never see your payment details.

Clicking & Counter

How clicks and the live number work.

What happens when I click?

It adds 1 to the global total, to your country's total, and to today's total. Each click always adds exactly one.

Is the number really live?

Yes. It comes from a shared real-time database, so you see other people's clicks as they arrive. A slow connection can cause a short delay.

What is "Clicks Today"?

The number of clicks since 00:00 UTC today. It resets at UTC midnight, wherever you are.

What are golden clicks and combos?

Just for fun. About 1 in 50 clicks sparkles gold, and quick successive clicks show a combo. They're visual only, and every click still adds exactly one. Your golden-click count and sound setting are kept only in your browser.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Use the small speaker button at the bottom-right of the CLICK button on the Home page. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

How do you keep the count fair?

Each click can only add one, there's a short pause between clicks from the same browser, and we use rate-limiting and bot-detection tools to help. No system is perfect, so we can't promise the number is free of automated clicks.

Countries & Leaderboard

How countries are credited and ranked.

How are countries ranked?

By the total clicks credited to each country. The Home page shows the top five, and the Leaderboard page lists every country, updated live.

Can I choose my country?

No, it's detected automatically. If you use a VPN or proxy, your clicks may be credited to a different country.

What if my country can't be detected?

The click still counts toward the global and daily totals, but it doesn't appear in the country rankings.

What is the Live Clicks list?

The most recent few clicks, showing the country and how long ago they happened. Older entries are overwritten, so no individual click history is kept.

Milestones

The big round numbers we're counting toward.

How is the next milestone chosen?

It's the next round number: every 100 clicks up to 1,000; every 1,000 up to 10,000; every 10,000 up to 100,000; every 100,000 up to 1,000,000; and every 1,000,000 after that. The progress bar on the Home page shows how close we are, and the Milestones page shows the big numbers ahead.

What happens when a milestone is reached?

If you have the page open, you'll see a short celebration with confetti and a banner. The Milestones page shows the date each big milestone was reached (in UTC).

Embed Widget

Put the live counter on your own site.

How do I embed the counter?

Open the Embed page, pick a size, copy the snippet and paste it into your site's HTML.

Do embedded clicks count?

Yes. They add to the same global and country totals as clicks on the main site.

Does the widget show ads or use cookies?

It shows no ads and loads no Ko-fi or Google Fonts content. It uses the same Firebase and reCAPTCHA tools as the main site, and may keep a random rate-limiting ID in the visitor's browser storage. See section 9 of the Privacy Policy.

Sponsorship

Featuring your brand at a milestone.

Can I sponsor a milestone?

The Top Countries placement (on the Home page and the Leaderboard page) is available now. Milestone placements open as the counter approaches each 1,000,000 milestone; email us to register interest. See the Sponsor page for details. Pricing is on request: sponsor@clickwiththeworld.fun.

Are sponsors labeled?

Yes. An active sponsor placement is marked with a small "Sponsored" label. Open placements read "Sponsor this spot".

Privacy & Data

What is and isn't stored.

What data do you collect?

Aggregate counts, a country code, and a random browser ID used to limit rapid clicking. Never your IP address, name or email with a click. See the Privacy Policy.

Do you use cookies?

We set no cookies ourselves; we use your browser's storage for a few small settings. Third-party services such as Google reCAPTCHA and AdSense may use cookies. See sections 3 to 5 of the Privacy Policy.

How do I reset my browser ID?

Clear your browser's site data for clickwiththeworld.fun.

Who do I ask about privacy?

Email hello@clickwiththeworld.fun or use the Contact page.
